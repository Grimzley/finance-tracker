Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 09/15/2025
BalancedBudget values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our finance management platform.
Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
  • Account Information: Name, email address, and login credentials when you register an account.
  • Financial Data: Transactions, budgets, account balances, and other finance-related details that you input.
  • Usage Data: Log files, IP address, device information, and browser type for security and analytics.
How We Use Information
We use the collected information to:
  • Provide and improve our financial management services
  • Secure user accounts and prevent fraud
  • Generate insights, budgets, and reports for your use
  • Communicate with you regarding your account or service updates
We do not sell or rent your personal or financial information to third parties.
Data Storage & Security
  • All sensitive data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption.
  • Access to your information is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • While we take strong measures to protect your data, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Third-Party Services
We may use third-party providers (e.g., hosting, analytics, or payment processors). These providers may process your data as necessary to support our services, subject to their own privacy policies.
Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
  • Access and request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Effective Date.”
